# Compliance universe — India v0 (Central + Maharashtra; 4 sector packs)

This is the auditor's map the product is built around. It drives the content packs (tasks
T06–T08), the applicability engine (T05), the prototype and the demo data.

> **Content status — read before using in front of a customer.** This register was drafted by
> AI acting as a compliance auditor (knowledge to mid-2026 plus spot checks in Oct 2026). Every
> item is *pending SME review*. Items marked **verify** contain a specific date, threshold or
> form number that must be confirmed from the cited source before a pack is published. The
> product enforces this: unreviewed obligations show a "Draft — pending SME review" badge.

## 1. What changed recently (and why the content model is versioned)

| Change | Effect on the register | Source to confirm |
|---|---|---|

Thresholds move (factory: 20 workers with power / 40 without; contract-labour licensing at 50). Wages definition with the 50% rule changes PF/gratuity/bonus bases. FTE gratuity after 1 year. | **DPDP Act 2023 + DPDP Rules 2025** (notified 13–14 Nov 2025); Board live; consent managers ~Nov 2026; **full compliance 13 May 2027**. | Every tenant gets a DPDP programme obligation; hospitals are high-risk (health data). Stridenity itself must comply (processor). | meity.gov.in |
| **Income-tax Act, 2025** effective 1 Apr 2026 (new form numbering under Income-tax Rules 2026). | TDS/ITR obligations re-cited; form numbers are pack data. | incometax.gov.in |
| **Boilers Act, 2025** effective 1 May 2025 (replaced the 1923 Act). | Boiler obligation re-cited. | dpiit.gov.in |
| **BOCW Act subsumed** — construction worker welfare cess now under the Code on Social Security. | Construction cess obligation re-cited. | labour.gov.in |
| **Jan Vishwas (Amendment of Provisions) Bill, 2026** — further decriminalisation. | Penalty texts may change; penalty data is versioned with `effective_from`. | prsindia.org |

**Design consequence:** every obligation carries `law`, `effective_from/to`, `source_url`,
`verified_on`, `review` status and a version. A regulatory change (FR-33) produces a pack diff
that tenants adopt through a workflow — the register is never silently edited.

## 2. How applicability works (auditor's logic)

The register for a unit = every obligation in the enabled packs whose `applies` rule is true for
that unit's **profile facts**. Facts are asked in onboarding (each with *why we ask*); key
thresholds an auditor always checks:

| Threshold | Triggers |
|---|---|
| ≥ 1 employee | Wage payment dates, registers, display, PT (MH) |
| ≥ 5 (MH) | Labour Welfare Fund |
| ≥ 10 | ESI, OSH establishment registration, POSH IC, gratuity, maternity benefit, health check-ups |
| ≥ 20 | EPF, bonus, grievance redressal committee; **factory licence with power** |
| ≥ 40 | Factory licence without power |
| ≥ 50 | Crèche; contractor licensing threshold for contractors |
| ≥ 300 | Standing orders, retrenchment permission (IR Code) |
| ≥ 250 / 500 | Safety officer (construction / factory) — verify |
| Equipment | Boiler, air receiver, lifting tackle, lift, DG set, HT installation, X-ray, USG |
| Activities | Hazardous waste, BMW, plastic packaging, packaged goods, weighing in trade, RERA project, construction work |
| Legal form | Companies Act vs LLP Act filings |

Proximity rule: when a fact is within 10% (or 2 workers) of a threshold, the dashboard shows
"EPF will apply at 20 employees — you are at 18".

## 3. Sources of obligations an MSME forgets (where auditors find most gaps)

1. **Licence/consent conditions** (CTO typically 30–80 conditions; BMW authorisation; AERB
   licence conditions; drug licence conditions) → FR-24 extracts them as obligations.
2. **Event-driven duties** — joiner registration, accident intimation, change in occupier/manager,
   machine added (USG/X-ray), breach intimation, F&F in 2 working days.
3. **Contractor duties of the principal employer.**
4. **Continuous duties** — registers, notices, crèche, first-aid, safety officer, fire systems —
   verified by periodic self-check instances with photo evidence.
5. **Renewals with long lead times** — CTO (apply ≥ 120 days ahead), AERB, PC-PNDT (30 days),
   nursing home registration, drug licence.

## 5. Evidence standards per obligation type (what the checker must see)

| Type | Minimum evidence to close |
|---|---|
| Payment (PF, ESI, TDS, GST, PT, LWF, cess) | Working/return file + challan with reference no. + bank debit proof |
| Return / filing | Final filed copy + acknowledgement / ARN / SRN |
| Renewal / licence | Application acknowledgement **and** later the renewed licence (instance stays "Applied — awaiting grant" until uploaded) |
| Continuous duty | Dated photo or register extract per self-check period |
| Training / drill | Plan, attendance sheet (signed), photos, report |
| Testing / examination | Certificate from competent person / recognised lab, with validity |
| Event intimation | Copy of intimation + proof of delivery / portal acknowledgement |
